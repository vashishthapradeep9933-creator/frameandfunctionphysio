01 Who is the lead physiotherapist at Frame & Function?

Dr. Gargi Jaimini (BPT, RUHS) is the founder and chief physiotherapist at Frame & Function Physiotherapy Clinic. She provides dedicated one-to-one, evidence-based physical therapy for sports injuries, orthopedic conditions, post-surgical recovery, and neuromuscular rehabilitation.

02 What conditions and therapies are offered?

We treat back pain, cervical/neck pain, sciatica, frozen shoulder, knee arthritis, ligament sprains, tennis elbow, slip disc, posture correction, and post-surgery rehabilitation. Advanced therapies include IASTM, Cupping Therapy, Kinesio Taping, Manual Joint Mobilization, Dry Needling, and Custom Exercise Therapy.

03 Do I need a doctor's prescription before visiting?

No prescription is required. You can consult directly. If you have any previous MRI scans, X-rays, or physician prescriptions, please bring them along to help tailor your personalized assessment.

04 Where is the clinic located and what are the timings?

We are conveniently located at Shop No. 44, Sundar Nagar, Karni Palace Road, Panchyawala, Jaipur 302034 (Near Sharma Dental, easily accessible from Vaishali Nagar & Sirsi Road). Timing is 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Monday to Sunday) by prior appointment.

05 How many sessions will I need, and is treatment painful?

Session counts vary depending on acute vs. chronic condition and recovery milestones. Our gentle, progressive approach stays strictly within your comfort threshold, ensuring therapeutic relief without undue pain.

06 How do I book an appointment with Dr. Gargi?

You can instantly book your appointment by calling or sending a message on WhatsApp at +91 91165 51222. All sessions are 1-on-1 to give you uninterrupted personal care.
